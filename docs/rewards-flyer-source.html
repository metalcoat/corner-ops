Program terms. Corner Deli Rewards (the "Program") is offered by Corner Deli, 828 Morris St, Ogdensburg, NY, and is not yet available; it will begin with the launch of Corner Deli's new online ordering, and the terms in effect at launch will govern. Membership is free and requires a Corner Deli customer account with a valid mobile phone number. Earning. Points are earned on qualifying food purchases only, after discounts, at 1 point per $1 for in-store and phone orders and 1.1 points per $1 for online orders, calculated to one tenth of a point. No points are earned on sales tax, tips, delivery or service fees, gift card purchases, items purchased with points or a reward, or any discounted or promotional item, including Jumbo Thursday. Double points apply to orders scheduled for pickup or delivery on Mondays and Tuesdays between 10:30 AM and 2:00 PM Eastern. Phone orders earn points only for the account matching the caller ID of the incoming call; blocked or unidentified numbers do not earn. Points post when the order is sent to the kitchen or completed, and are removed if the order is voided, cancelled or refunded. Jumbo card. Ten (10) Jumbo 16" pizzas (thick or thin) purchased at regular price earn one free plain Jumbo Thin 16" pizza; it may be upgraded to a Jumbo Thick for the current price difference (currently $1.00). Toppings and add-ons are charged at regular price. Redeeming. Rewards cover the regular price of the listed item or one regular portion of the listed add-on only; additional toppings, sizes and modifications are charged at regular price. To redeem online you must be signed in to your account. To redeem in store or by phone, you must provide the one-time code texted to the mobile number on your account; staff cannot redeem points without it. Points cannot be redeemed through the automated phone ordering line. One reward per item. Points and rewards have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for cash or gift cards, cannot be combined with other discounts unless stated, and may not be sold, transferred or shared between accounts. Expiration. Points expire if the account has no qualifying purchase for six (6) consecutive months. General. Reward point values, reward items and earning rates are subject to change. Corner Deli may change, suspend or end the Program, or any reward, at any time, with or without notice, and may suspend accounts and forfeit points in cases of suspected fraud, abuse or violation of these terms. Message and data rates may apply to verification texts. Limited to participating Corner Deli location. Void where prohibited. Menu items subject to availability. All prices include New York State and local sales tax.
